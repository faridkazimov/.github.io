I am an AI Engineer specializing in **Computer Vision** and **Generative AI (LLMs & NLP)**, focused on building **production-ready, scalable AI systems** for real-world applications.

In **Computer Vision**, I develop and deploy robust detection systems using **YOLO-based architectures**, with a focus on performance in challenging conditions such as occlusion, dense scenes, and varying lighting. I work across the full pipeline — from data preparation and model training to optimization and low-latency deployment.

In **Generative AI (LLMs & NLP)**, I design **RAG-based systems** and **LLM-powered agents** that enable reliable interaction with unstructured data. My work focuses on prompt design, context management, and evaluation, as well as building scalable retrieval pipelines. I also have experience in NLP tasks such as text classification, NER, semantic search, and information extraction.

I also have experience working with **cloud-based AI systems on Microsoft Azure**, where I deploy and manage AI solutions in production.

I am passionate about building systems that are not only accurate, but also **efficient, reliable, and ready for real-world use**.
